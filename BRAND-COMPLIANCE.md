# Bê Brand Academy — Brand Compliance Notes

Based on the logo guideline supplied by Bê Brand.

## Official palette

- Brand red `#C23B27`
- Brand cream `#DED2C1`
- Brand charcoal `#3D3E38`
- Brand white `#FFFFFF`

The dark website *canvas* uses `#0B0D0C` by the owner's request; this is a UI background, not a recoloring of any official logo.

## Logo usage

- The site references the complete, official **symbol + Bê Brand type** logo (portrait lockup).
- Image assets are preserved in `public/logos/logo-light.png`, `public/logos/logo-dark.png`, plus matching SVG exports. The PNG assets use transparent backgrounds.
- No distortion, stretching, rotating, skewing, blur, shadow, outlining, 3D extrusion or gradient effect is applied to logo artwork.
- The header maintains a cream-toned surface for the approved charcoal variant to remain legible in Dark Mode; the red logo displays in Light Mode.
- The footer uses the red logo on the near-black canvas; the About section showcases the same official mark on cream.
- The hero's animated sculpture is a standalone **non-logo** shape; it does not alter the Bê Brand icon.
- Header logo sizing has been reduced for editorial balance, with open whitespace around the asset.

## Fonts

- Clash Display is specified by the logo guideline for headings; for Vietnamese display text, Be Vietnam Pro is prioritized as a compatible fallback so tone marks are not clipped.
- Roboto Slab is used for body text.
- Font files are not included in the package; font references load from public font services.
